INSIDE AN ANALYSIS

Six angles on every stock

Other tools hand you charts and leave the thinking to you. Velu does the work, reaches a conclusion, and shows you exactly how it got there.

◈

Three agents, one verdict

A macro strategist, a quant, and a tail-risk analyst each take a position. You get Buy, Hold or Sell with a conviction level and a 12-month target — plus the argument against it.

◪

Five years of trajectory

Revenue, earnings and margins year by year, with the growth rate and whether margins are expanding or compressing. Velu compares that trajectory directly against the price move.

⚠

Why it actually fell

Velu reads the latest news on every company and explains the real catalyst behind the selloff — with the sources linked, so you can judge whether the fear is justified.

⚖

Sector context

Did the whole industry fall, or just this name? Velu compares each stock against a dozen sector peers, so you know if you're looking at rotation or a company-specific problem.

◎

Velu vs Wall Street

See exactly where Velu's price target diverges from the analyst consensus, and by how much. Then send the call to anyone on WhatsApp or X in one tap.

◉

Two records, both honest

Velu scores every call it makes against real prices — no cherry-picking. And you keep your own list: track only the calls you actually care about, add or remove them any time.

TWO DISCOVERY ENGINES

Mispricing works both ways

A stock can be mispriced because the price fell below the business — or because the business is improving faster than the price. Velu hunts both.

⚡

Punished

Good companies the market oversold

Scans 176 major US stocks and ranks the ones trading furthest below their own highs and moving averages — then checks whether the fundamentals justify the fall. When they don't, that gap is the opportunity.
